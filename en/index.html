🚀 New to Plumya?

  1. Join the server → Connect to Plumya.net
  2. Choose a race → Pick a character class that suits your playstyle
  3. Fill your soul → Hunt mobs to maintain your daily 160 soul energy
  4. Enter a Boss → Fight with enough soul to earn rare loot drops
  5. Rank up → Complete quests and begin your Legendary journey
