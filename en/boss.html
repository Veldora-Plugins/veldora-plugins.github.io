⚔️ Epic Boss Encounters

Review the following rules and requirements before entering the Boss arena.

🛡️ Entry Modes & Participation

Choose your Boss and initiate the duel through the interface.

  • Solo (1 Player): For those who wish to prove their individual strength.
  • Group (4 Players): Challenging battles requiring teamwork.
  • Party & Guild: Enter with your friends or fellow guild members.
  • Find Player: Use the matchmaking system to form a 4-man squad.

📜 Combat Rules

  • Queue System: Boss rooms are unique instances. Every team must wait for the current battle to conclude before entering.
  • Boss Selection: You have the freedom to select which Boss you want to duel from the menu.

💎 Loot and Drops

Victory rewards are reserved for the worthy.

  • Soul Requirement: You must possess at least 40 SOUL points to collect drops upon the Boss's defeat.

  • Note: Players with less than 40 Soul will not receive any loot.

  • Item Types:

  • Race-Specific: Rare items tailored to your specific class (Elf, Dwarf, etc.).

  • Universal: General equipment with a chance to drop for any player.
