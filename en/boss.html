⚔️ Epic Boss Encounters

The most challenging and rewarding content in Veldora: the Boss arena. Every Boss encounter offers rare loot exclusively to prepared players.

⚔️ Boss System — Overview
Min. Players1 (Solo)
Max. Players4 (Group)
Soul RequirementMin. 40 🔮
Instance TypeUnique (Instance)
Loot TypesRace-Specific, Universal
Queue SystemNext group enters after battle ends

Review the following rules and requirements carefully before entering the Boss arena. Entering unprepared may mean leaving with no loot at all.

🛡️ Entry Modes & Participation

Choose your Boss and initiate the duel through the interface.

ModePlayersDescription
Solo1For those who wish to prove their individual strength.
Group4Challenging battles requiring full teamwork.
Party & Guild2–4Enter with your friends or fellow guild members.
Find Player4Use the matchmaking system to form a 4-man squad.

📜 Combat Rules

  • Unique Instance: Each Boss room is an independent instance. While one team is fighting, others wait in queue.
  • Boss Selection: You have the freedom to select any Boss from the menu to challenge.

💎 Loot and Drops

⚠️ Soul Requirement

You must possess at least 40 Soul points to collect drops upon the Boss's defeat.

Players with less than 40 Soul will not receive any loot.

Victory rewards are reserved for the worthy.

Loot TypeDescription
🧬 Race-SpecificRare equipment that only drops for your specific race (Elf, Dwarf, etc.)
🌐 UniversalGeneral equipment with a chance to drop for any race
