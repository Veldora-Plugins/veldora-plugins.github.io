🛡️ Character Classes

All features, advantages, and disadvantages of the five different character classes.

🧝 Elf

Guardians of nature, experts in speed and range.

  • Speed and Ranged Attacks: Naturally faster movement and deals extra damage with bows.
  • Forest Survival: Regenerates health while in Forest biomes.
  • Weakness (Fragility): Low resistance against physical attacks.

🧔 Dwarf

Known for their durable build and mining abilities.

  • Durability: Has 16 base health and hits hard with axes.
  • Natural Speed: Possesses a permanent "Haste" effect.
  • Mining: Gains bonuses from mining resources.

🧛 Vampire

Night hunters that drain the life energy of their opponents.

  • Life Steal: Regenerates health on attacks and features a combo system.
  • Weakness (Sun): Burns in daylight unless at a high rank.
  • Critical: Taking damage from Saints blocks life stealing abilities.

👼 Saint

The heart and healer of the team.

  • Team Healer: Continuously heals allies in the vicinity.
  • Special Weakness: Takes extra damage from Vampires.
  • Damage: General damage output is low.

🗿 Giant

A tank class that stands like an indestructible wall.

  • Massive Health Pool: 40 base health and natural damage reduction.
  • Weakness (Slowness): Strength and durability cause very slow movement.
