🛡️ Character Classes

There are five different races in the Veldora universe. Each race has unique passive abilities, health values, and combat styles. Your chosen race affects not only your gameplay but also the loot you receive from Boss battles.

📌Race selection is permanent. Contact a server administrator to change it. Review all races carefully before making your choice.

🧝 Elf

🧝 Elf
RoleRanged · Speed
Base Health10 ❤️ (Normal)
StrengthsSpeed boost, Bow damage
PassiveHealth regen in Forest biomes
WeaknessPhysical attacks

Guardians of nature; unmatched in speed and ranged combat. They perform best in open areas and forest biomes.

  • ⚡ Speed & Ranged Attacks: Naturally faster movement and deals extra damage with bows.
  • 🌿 Forest Survival: Passively regenerates health while in Forest biomes.
  • ⚠️ Weakness — Fragility: Low resistance against physical melee attacks.

🧔 Dwarf

🧔 Dwarf
RoleCombat · Miner
Base Health16 ❤️ (High)
StrengthsDurability, Axe damage
PassivePermanent Haste, Mining bonus
Weakness—

With their iron-tough bodies and tireless energy, Dwarves are reliable fighters and resource gatherers in any environment.

  • 🛡️ Durability: Has 16 base health and deals heavy blows with axes.
  • 🏃 Natural Speed: Permanently active "Haste" effect makes them more agile.
  • ⛏️ Mining: Gains extra resources and bonuses from mining ore blocks.

🧛 Vampire

🧛 Vampire
RoleAttacker · Life Steal
Base Health10 ❤️ (Normal)
StrengthsLife steal, Combo system
PassiveHealth regen on attack
WeaknessSunlight, Saint damage

Night hunters who survive by draining the life energy of their enemies. Powerful in prolonged fights but carries serious disadvantages under certain conditions.

  • 🩸 Life Steal: Drains health from enemies on each hit and gains combo bonuses on consecutive attacks.
  • ☀️ Weakness — Sun: At low ranks, takes damage from sunlight during daytime.
  • ✝️ Critical Weakness: Life stealing ability is temporarily disabled when taking damage from a Saint.

👼 Saint

👼 Saint
RoleSupport · Healer
Base Health10 ❤️ (Normal)
StrengthsTeam healing aura
PassiveContinuously heals nearby allies
WeaknessExtra damage from Vampires, Low attack

The heart and lifeline of the team. Saints are designed not to fight alone, but to keep their allies standing.

  • 💊 Team Healer: Passively and continuously heals allied players in the nearby area.
  • 🧛 Special Weakness: Takes more than normal damage from attacks made by the Vampire race.
  • ⚔️ Low Damage: General damage output is the lowest among all races.

🗿 Giant

🗿 Giant
RoleTank · Frontline
Base Health40 ❤️ (Very High)
StrengthsMassive health, Damage reduction
PassiveNatural damage reduction effect
WeaknessVery slow movement

Standing like an indestructible fortress, the Giant has the highest survival capacity of all races. They hold the frontline, absorbing damage and distracting enemies.

  • 💪 Massive Health Pool: 40 base health and a natural damage reduction passive make them unmatched in long battles.
  • 🐌 Weakness — Slowness: As the price of high durability, they move very slowly.
