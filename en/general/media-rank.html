🎥 Media Creator (Media Rank)

We support content creators who share their adventures in the Veldora universe with their followers! If you want to be a part of our community and benefit from exclusive advantages, check out the requirements below.

💡 About Application

If you believe you meet the requirements, you can contact us via the "Media Application" category on Discord.

📜 Requirements and Responsibilities

To maintain server quality and ensure stability, we expect the following criteria:

  • 🎂 Age Requirement: Content creators must be at least 16 years old.
  • 📅 Content Consistency: You must share at least 4 videos or host 4 live streams per week.
  • ⏳ Stream Duration: Each live stream must have a minimum continuous duration of 1 hour.
  • 🛡️ Ethics: No insulting, belittling, or toxic behavior towards other players is allowed in streams or videos.
  • 🚫 Advertising Ban: While holding the Media rank in Veldora, it is strictly forbidden to promote or advertise other Minecraft servers.

🎁 Media Rank Benefits and Rewards

Special support packages we offer to our content creators:

  • 🎖️ Media Rank: A unique "Media" rank and color will be assigned to you in-game and on our Discord server.
  • 💰 Weekly Site Credits: Content creators who meet the requirements will receive a fixed amount of Site Credits every week.
  • 🎟️ Giveaway Coupons: Special Credit Coupons will be provided for you to distribute to your followers and grow your audience.
  • 🚀 Social Media Support: Your high-quality content will be supported by being shared on our official channels.

⚖️ Rank Revocation

🛑 Important Notice

The Media rank and its privileges will be revoked without question in the following cases:

  • Identification of false age or identity information.
  • Failure to meet the weekly content quota.
  • Violation of server rules or ethical guidelines.
  • Advertising other servers.
