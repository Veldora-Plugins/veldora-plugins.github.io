❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

We have gathered the most common questions and answers for players who have just joined the Veldora universe or are curious about our systems.

💡 Tip

If you cannot find your question here, you can use the /support command in-game or reach out to us via Discord.

🌀 Soul and Energy System

Details about the Soul system, which is the primary resource for your in-game activities.

  • 🕯️ My Souls are empty, what should I do? To continue your activities, you can gain souls by hunting Zombies and Skeletons. Additionally, at 06:00 every morning, the souls of all players are automatically refilled to 160.

🎭 Races and Gameplay

Choices that affect your character development and the rewards you receive.

  • 🧬 Which race should I choose? Every race has its own unique advantages. It is recommended to choose the one that best fits your playstyle. Remember, the race you choose directly impacts the types and rates of items dropped from Boss battles.

🏆 Ranks and Progression

Information about the journey to the peak of Veldora.

  • ✨ How do I reach the Legendary rank? To rank up, you need to complete daily quests regularly. Once you reach the Legendary rank, you gain the right to enter the "Blessing Zone," one of the most exclusive areas of the server.

💬 Communication and Chat

Technical details about our server's unique multi-language support.

  • 🌐 Why can't I see messages written in other languages? Veldora features a Multi-Language System. If your language selection is set to English, you will only see English-speaking players to prevent chat clutter. To see conversations in other languages or to speak in those languages, you must update your language selection.

📖 Guides

Don't forget to visit the Guide category in the left menu to get more in-depth information about the systems!
