📜 Veldora Server Rules

To maintain game quality and ensure a fair environment for everyone, the following rules are mandatory.

🚫 General Conduct

  1. Respect and Etiquette: Be respectful to other players and staff. Swearing, insults, and derogatory remarks (Toxic behavior) are prohibited.
  2. Spam and Advertising: Spamming the chat or advertising other servers is strictly forbidden.
  3. Language Selection: We have a Language-Specific Chat System. Please select English if you are speaking English, and Turkish if you are speaking Turkish.

Cheating and Exploits

Exploiting server bugs or using cheats (killaura, fly, x-ray, etc.) will result in a permanent ban.

⚔️ In-Game Rules

  • TPA Trapping: Killing players by luring them with /tpa is prohibited.
  • Scamming: Deceiving other players in trade transactions is forbidden. Please use the Auction House system for secure trading.
