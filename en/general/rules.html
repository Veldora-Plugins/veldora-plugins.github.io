📜 Veldora Server & Community Rules

To ensure a fair, respectful, and sustainable gaming experience within the Veldora universe, all users must adhere to the following regulations. By joining our servers (Minecraft & Discord), you are deemed to have accepted these rules in their entirety.

💡 Information

If you have any questions regarding the rules or wish to report a violation, please do not hesitate to contact our staff team.

🚫 General Behavior and Ethics

Respect is the foundation of our community.

  • 🛡️ Respect and Etiquette: Profanity, slang, insults, and any form of derogatory language towards other players, staff members, or server values are strictly prohibited. Personalizing conflicts and creating a toxic environment will result in punishment.
  • 📢 Spam and Chat Order: Filling the chat with meaningless characters, shouting in all capital letters (CAPS), or sending the same message repeatedly is forbidden.
  • 🌍 Global Language Policy: Our server features a Language-Specific Chat System. Please use the Turkish channels for Turkish and English channels for English.
  • 🚫 Advertising: Promoting other Minecraft servers, Discord addresses, or commercial websites (including via private messages) is grounds for a permanent ban.

⚔️ In-Game Mechanic Rules

Rules established to ensure fair competition and prevent the exploitation of labor.

  • 🪤 TPA and Teleportation Safety: Luring players into lava, voids, closed traps, or lethal mechanisms via the /tpa command is considered "TPA Trapping" and is strictly forbidden.
  • 🤝 Trading and Scams: Defrauding other parties during player trades, stealing items, or failing to provide promised payments is prohibited. For your safety, using the /ah (Auction House) system is highly recommended.
  • 🏗️ Claim and Land Violations: Surrounding other players' claimed lands with walls, blocking entrances, or building disturbing structures near their territory is forbidden.
  • 📦 Item Refunds: Except for technical issues caused by the server, items that are stolen, lost, or burned in lava will not be refunded.

🛑 Cheating and Bug Exploitation (Zero Tolerance)

  • Cheat Software: The use of Killaura, Fly, X-ray, Auto-clicker, or any 3rd party software that provides an unfair advantage will result in a permanent ban.
  • Bug Exploitation: Users who discover an in-game glitch (Bug) and exploit it for their own gain instead of reporting it will be heavily penalized. Reporting bugs to staff may be rewarded.

💬 Discord Community Rules

Rules applicable for maintaining order within our social community.

  • 📌 Categorization: Ensure all messages are posted in the appropriate channels. Send help requests to #support and media content to the #media channel.
  • 👤 Privacy and Personal Data: Sharing any user's personal data (Full name, photo, address, phone number) without their consent (Doxing) is strictly prohibited.
  • 🔊 Voice Channels: Making excessive noise (ear-rape), abusing music bots, or disturbing others in voice rooms is forbidden.
  • 📛 Identity and Profile: Using inappropriate profile pictures, offensive usernames, or misleading roles is prohibited.

⚖️ Authority and Enforcement

📝 Final Note

The Veldora staff team reserves the right to intervene and penalize actions that disrupt community peace, even if they are not explicitly listed in the rules above. Rules may be updated over time; it is the player's responsibility to stay informed.
