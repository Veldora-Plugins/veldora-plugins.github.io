📜 Veldora Server and Community Rules

The following is the rule and penalty schedule applied to maintain order in the Veldora universe. By joining our server, you are deemed to have read these rules and accepted full responsibility for your actions.

⚠️ Disclaimer and Critical Warning

  • No Claim System: Our server does not feature a land claim system. The safety of your structures and items is entirely your own responsibility.
  • TPA & Trading Responsibility: Accepting /tpa requests and conducting manual trades (dropping items) are done at your own risk. TPA trapping or trade scams (scamming) are NOT punishable offenses. You must accept the risk of losing items when interacting with strangers.
  • No Refunds: No items lost through these methods will be refunded by the staff team. Use the /ah (Auction House) system for secure trading.

🚫 1. Chat and Ethics (Mute Schedule)

  1. Profanity and Slang: Using rude or offensive language towards players or values.
    • Penalty: 15 Minutes - 2 Hours Mute
  2. Severe Insults: Heavy attacks on personal or family values.
    • Penalty: 1 Day - 7 Days Mute (Permanent Ban on repeat)
  3. Spam and Flood: Polluting the chat with fast or meaningless messages.
    • Penalty: 10 Minutes - 1 Hour Mute
  4. Capital Letters (Caps Lock): Shouting by constantly typing in all caps.
    • Penalty: Warning / 5 Minutes Mute
  5. Language Misuse: Speaking English in Turkish channels or vice versa.
    • Penalty: Warning / 15 Minutes Mute
  6. Politics and Religion: Opening sensitive topics that polarize the community.
    • Penalty: 2 Hours - 12 Hours Mute
  7. Advertising: Promotion of other Minecraft servers or commercial entities.
    • Penalty: Permanent (IP) Ban

⚔️ 2. In-Game Rules

  1. TPA and Trading Security: Setting TPA traps or deceiving players during manual trades is considered an in-game strategy/risk. No penalties are applied for these actions.
  2. Bug Abuse: Gaining an unfair advantage by exploiting game glitches.
    • Penalty: 30 Days Ban - Permanent Ban (All data may be reset)
  3. Multi-Accounting: Using alt accounts to farm Buds or bypass penalties.
    • Penalty: Permanent ban for all associated accounts.

🚨 3. Cheating (Zero Tolerance)

  1. 3rd Party Software: Killaura, Fly, X-ray, Auto-Clicker, or any software that provides an unfair advantage.
    • Penalty: Permanent Ban

💬 4. Discord and Privacy Rules

  1. Doxing (Personal Data): Sharing someone's phone number, real name, or photo without consent.
    • Penalty: Permanent ban from all platforms.
  2. Voice Channel Disturbance: Abusing microphones or music bots.
    • Penalty: Mute or Kick from Discord.

📝 Final Note

Veldora management reserves the right to change these rules without prior notice. It is the player's responsibility to stay updated.
