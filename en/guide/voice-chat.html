🎙️ Voice Chat System

In the world of Veldora, you can communicate with other players in real-time, strategize with your guild, or conduct trades using your voice.

💡 Essential Information

To use this system, you must install the Simple Voice Chat mod on your computer. If the mod is not installed, you will not be able to hear or speak to other players.

🛠️ Setup Guide

Follow these steps to start vocal communication.

  1. Download Mod: Download the appropriate version (Fabric/Forge) for your Minecraft client from Modrinth.
  2. Installation: Place the downloaded .jar file into the mods folder within your Minecraft directory.
  3. Connection: When you log into the server, if you see a microphone icon in the bottom-left corner, the system is successfully activated.

⌨️ Basic Controls and Keybinds

  • 🎤 Push-to-Talk: The default key is V. Hold it down to speak.
  • ⚙️ Settings Menu: Press V (or your designated menu key) to adjust volume levels, microphone sensitivity, and speaker settings.
  • 🔇 Mute Toggle: Use the M key to quickly mute or unmute your microphone.

🎭 Features

  • 🔊 Proximity Voice: As players move away from you, their voice gets quieter; as they get closer, it gets louder.
  • 🛡️ Guild Groups: You can create private voice groups with your /guild members to communicate regardless of distance.
  • 🎵 Acoustic Effects: Your voice will echo in caves or sound muffled underwater depending on the environment.

🛑 Rules

Harassing other players, screaming, or using insults through voice chat is strictly forbidden and will result in a ban from the system.
