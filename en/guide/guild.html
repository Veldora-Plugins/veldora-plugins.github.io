🛡️ Guild System

Surviving alone in the Plumya universe is challenging. Build your own community, make allies, and climb to the top with your guild.

💡 Strategy

A strong guild enables you to obtain more loot with less effort in Boss battles.

🏰 Guild Creation and Management

Unity is strength.

  • 🚩 Founding a Guild: You can create your own guild using the /guild create <name> command in-game.
  • 👥 Member Management: Invite your friends with /guild invite <player> and authorize them by setting guild ranks.
  • 🏠 Guild Home: Establish a strategic base by setting a special center (Home) where guild members can teleport.

⚔️ Inter-Guild Competition (PvP)

Prove that you are the most powerful community in Plumya.

  • 🤺 Guild Duels: You can challenge other guilds and initiate the inter-guild war system.
  • 📊 Rankings: The players you defeat and the wars you win increase your guild points, moving you up the Guild Rankings (Top List).

🐲 Boss Battles and Cooperation

Be one heart against giant monsters.

  • 🤝 Fighting Together: You can head to Boss zones by forming groups with your guild members.
  • 💎 Shared Loot: Rewards from bosses killed with the guild are optimized and distributed based on guild members' race and soul levels.
  • 🛡️ Support Mechanics: You can take down tougher enemies by protecting each other with your guild members during Boss battles.

⚖️ Guild Rules

🛑 Important Notice

Using insults, profanity, or terms against community rules in the guild name will result in the guild being closed without question.
