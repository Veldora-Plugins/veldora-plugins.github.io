🏆 Rank System and Progression

Here are the details of the rank system representing your prestige and power in the Veldora universe.

💡 Progression Guide

To earn XP and advance to the next rank, you must accept missions through the Server Guild.

📊 Rank Requirements

The following table lists the required XP amounts and system priorities for each rank:

RankNameRequired XPPriority
FF0 XP0
EE250 XP1
DD500 XP2
CC750 XP3
BB1000 XP4
AA2000 XP5
SLegends4000 XP6

🛡️ Farming XP via Guild Quests

The only way to rise in Veldora is to work for the guild.

  • 📜 Mission Center: The Server Guild is the main source of missions where you can earn XP.
  • ⚔️ Mission Process: Every mission you take scales with your rank's difficulty and adds rank XP to your account upon completion.
  • ✨ Legends Advantage: Upon reaching the highest rank, Legends, you gain access to exclusive zones and privileged markets.

🛑 Important Notice

Rank XP can only be obtained through guild missions; normal mob hunting or mining does not directly affect your rank level.
