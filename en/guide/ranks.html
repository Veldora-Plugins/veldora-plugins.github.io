🏆 Rank System and Progression

Here are the details of the progression system that determines your character's prestige and world access authority in the Veldora universe.

💡 Progression Guide

To earn XP and advance to the next level, you must accept missions through the Server Guild. As your level increases, the gates to new dimensions will open for you.

📊 Requirements and World Permissions

The following table lists the names and the critical access permissions granted by these levels:

NameRequired XPWorld Access

Legendary4000 XPAll Worlds

🛡️ Farming XP via Guild Quests

The only way to rise in Veldora is to work for the guild.

  • 📜 Mission Center: The Server Guild is the only official source where you can earn XP.
  • ⏱️ Quest Refresh Rate: Guild quests are automatically refreshed every 4 hours. It is recommended to keep track of this time for new missions.
  • ⚔️ Mission Process: Every mission you take scales with your rank's difficulty. Complete these missions faithfully to access new worlds.

🌎 Dimensional and World Access

  • 🔥 Nether Journey: You must reach at least D level to step into the dangerous lands of the Nether.
  • 🌌 End Exploration: Access to the End dimension, the dragon's lair, is granted only to players at B level and above.

🛑 Important Notice

If your level is insufficient, the system will repel you even if you use the portal to the respective world. Focus on guild missions to reach resources in other dimensions!
