⚔️ Game Systems

Details of the unique mechanics found in Veldora server.

👻 Soul System

The Soul system is the core energy source governing your activities.

  • Capacity: You start with a base limit of 160 Souls.
  • How to Gain: You can recover lost souls by killing Zombies and Skeletons.
  • Daily Reset: Your souls automatically reset to 160 every morning at 06:00 AM.
  • Important Note: The Soul system is linked to the Boss system. Participating in boss fights with insufficient souls may affect your rewards.

🧬 Race & Boss Interaction

Your chosen race defines not just your look, but your earnings too.

  • Dynamic Loot: The Race system is integrated with the Boss system. The items and drop rates from Bosses change depending on your chosen race.

🌍 Multi-Language System

Our server is globally structured.

  • Auto Translation: Messages from server plugins are displayed in your selected language.
  • Chat Separation: Players who select Turkish see only Turkish messages, and those who select English see only English messages.

🏆 Rank System

Your progression is based on quests.

  • Daily Quests: You progress by completing quests that refresh daily.
  • Legendary Rank: Completing quests moves you closer to the "Legendary" rank.
  • Blessing Zone: A special area accessible only to players who have reached the Legendary rank.

💰 Economy

  • Sell Menu: You can earn money by selling items. Item values are listed in the item descriptions in-game.
  • Auction House: Use the bidding system for secure trading with other players.
