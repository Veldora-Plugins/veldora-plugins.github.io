🌾 Farm & Mob Areas

Veldora features two dedicated resource-earning zones: the Mob Area and the Farm Area. Coins earned in both areas can be exchanged for various items at the server shop.

💡Both areas are open to everyone. No prerequisites required to participate.

⚔️ Mob Area

⚔️ Mob Area
Currency EarnedMob Coin 🪙
ActivityKilling mobs
UsageItem exchange

The Mob Area is a dedicated zone where you can earn Mob Coins by slaying monsters. The more mobs you kill, the more coins you accumulate.

  • 🗡️ Kill Mobs: Defeat monsters in the area to earn Mob Coins.
  • 🪙 Stack Coins: Accumulate coins to afford more valuable items.
  • 🛒 Exchange: Trade your Mob Coins for exclusive items at the server shop.

🌾 Farm Area

🌾 Farm Area
Currency EarnedFarm Coin 🌽
ActivityHarvesting wheat
UsageItem exchange

The Farm Area is an agricultural zone where you earn Farm Coins by harvesting wheat. Work the fields and watch your coins grow.

  • 🌱 Harvest: Break wheat in the farm area to earn Farm Coins.
  • 🌽 Stack Coins: Build up your coin balance from each harvest.
  • 🛒 Exchange: Trade your Farm Coins for exclusive items at the server shop.

🛒 Coin Exchange

Both coin types can be exchanged for various items at the server shop.
