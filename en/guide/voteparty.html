🎉 Vote Party

Vote Party is a community-driven event where all players collectively vote to trigger a big reward event. Once the counter fills up, a Piñata drops into the server!

🎉 Vote Party
TriggerVote counter reaching its goal
RewardPiñata 🪅
ParticipationAll online players
How to Vote/vote command

By voting for the server, you both contribute to filling the Vote Party counter and help the server grow. Every vote counts!

🪅 The Piñata

When the vote counter reaches its target, a Piñata drops into the server. Smash it open and grab the rewards inside!

🎊 How Does the Piñata Work?

When the counter fills, a piñata drops in-game. Hit the piñata to release the rewards hidden inside. Be quick — loot is limited!

🗳️ How to Vote

  1. Type /vote in-game.
  2. Follow the link and cast your vote for the server.
  3. Receive your personal vote reward in-game.
  4. Watch the counter fill up — the Piñata is coming!

❓ Why Should I Vote?

BenefitDescription
🪅 PiñataWhen the counter fills, a piñata with rewards drops for everyone
🎁 Personal RewardEarn an instant reward for every vote you cast
📈 Server GrowthHigher vote rankings attract more players to the server
